# Forgotten World Highway map: deep audit

Date: 2026-09-08
Target: `~/Documents/Claude/Projects/Forgotten World Highway/index.html`
Auditor: Claude Code, independent pass. Prior session log read last, per procedure 12.

Audited at 619 lines / 44,104 bytes. Now 219,073 bytes after fixes and the

## Probe (procedure 8, run first)

The downstream artefact is not the page, it is a day of driving. I planned a real
two-day Stratford to Taumarunui run for a self-contained Sprinter using only what
the page shows, and logged every question it failed to answer.

Questions the page could not answer:

1. Will the van fit through Moki Tunnel? No clearance figure anywhere.
2. Where is the next fuel? The page says "fuel up" at Stratford but never states
   that there is no fuel at all for the following 148 km.
3. Where do we fill fresh water or dump grey and black?
4. Is there cell coverage? The page itself needs coverage to draw its own map.
5. How long does the drive actually take? No time figures, only kilometres.
6. Where can we walk? Two walks exist in 22 stops, one of them two minutes long.
7. For the detour stops, how far is the actual drive, and where do we turn off?

Questions 1, 2 and 7 changed a day's plan and became findings B2, C4 and N1. All
seven are now answered on the page: 1 and 5 by the van essentials panel, 2 and 3 by
the fuel and dump station cards, 4 by the coverage card plus the offline rebuild,
6 by six walks and six lookouts, 7 by real drive distances and times.

## BLOCKING

### B1. Three markers were permanently unclickable. FIXED

Three POI pairs shared byte-identical coordinates, so the second marker of each pair
covered the first exactly. The lower marker could never receive a map click.

```
     0.0 m : Whangamōmona                    <-> Whangamōmona Camping Ground
     0.0 m : Tangarakau Gorge                <-> Tangarakau Gorge free camp
     0.0 m : Ōhura                           <-> Ōhura Cosmopolitan Club
```

Original reproduction, in the running page:

```js
document.elementFromPoint(rect.left+cp.x, rect.top+cp.y).className
// -> "pin pin-camping"
```

At Whangamōmona the topmost element was the campground pin, so the attraction popup
carrying the Republic of Whangamōmona story could not be opened from the map at all.
It was reachable only via the sidebar card, because that path calls
`markers[i].openPopup()` directly.

Fixed as data, not as a rendering hack:

- Whangamōmona Camping Ground moved to its OSM-verified location
  (-39.148664, 174.740265), 560 m from the hotel.
- Tangarakau Gorge free camp moved to a computed real route point at km 86.0
  (-38.99209, 174.82259). The gorge is a 12 km linear feature, so a single point was
  always arbitrary.
- Ōhura and Ōhura Cosmopolitan Club merged into one camping POI. The club's own
  coordinates could not be confirmed in OSM or Nominatim, and inventing them would
  have broken the project's accuracy rule.

Post-fix, all 29 markers enumerated rather than sampled, each centred at zoom 16 and
hit-tested with `elementFromPoint`:

```
{ tested: 29, blockedCount: 0, blocked: [] }
```

Closest surviving pair is 200 m (Forgotten World Adventures depot <-> Taumarunui),
and zero pairs are within 1 m. The elevation ribbon collision at km 61.7, 89.5,
109.4 and 148.6 is fixed separately by a fan-out that stacks co-located ticks
vertically; measured pixel duplicates after fan-out: `[]`.

### B2. `detourKm` was straight-line distance, presented as a driving detour. FIXED

`detourKm` was computed by projecting the POI onto the nearest point of the route
polyline. The popup rendered it as `· 6.9km detour`, which any reader takes as road
distance. Re-derived against OSRM:

```
Mount Damper Falls    straight-line  6.9 km  |  ACTUAL DRIVE  21.2 km (41 min)
Ohura                 straight-line  7.3 km  |  ACTUAL DRIVE  10.2 km (11 min)
Taumarunui Holiday Pk straight-line  4.5 km  |  ACTUAL DRIVE   5.3 km  (7 min)
Stratford Holiday Pk  straight-line  0.7 km  |  ACTUAL DRIVE   0.9 km  (2 min)
```

Mount Damper Falls was out by a factor of three. The page implied a 15 minute
diversion; the real cost is 42 km and about 1 h 22 min of driving on gravel, before
the walk itself.

The same projection artefact affected `km` for detour stops. Mount Damper Falls was
labelled "88.6 km from Stratford", but that was the perpendicular foot of the
projection, not a place on the highway.

Fixed: every detour now carries an OSRM drive distance and a drive time, and detour
popups read "off the highway near km X" rather than implying the stop sits at that
kilometre. Detours of 15 km or more render in the warning style. Verified that no
detour stop carries one figure without the other:

```
detourIncomplete: []
detours: Carrington Walkway 0.6km/1min, Stratford Holiday Park 0.9km/2min,
         Mount Damper Falls 21.2km/41min, Ohura 10.2km/11min,
         Te Maire Loop Track 1.6km/3min, Piriaka Lookout 17.8km/17min,
         Taumarunui Holiday Park 5.3km/7min,
         Forgotten World Adventures depot 0.9km/2min, Taumarunui 0.6km/2min,
         Taumarunui River Walk 0.3km/1min
```

### B3. The page needed live internet, on a road that has almost none. FIXED

Leaflet JS and CSS, Google Fonts and every map tile were fetched from CDNs at load
time. SH43 is one of the least covered stretches of state highway in the North
Island. The tool stopped working exactly where it is meant to be used, and if the
Leaflet CDN fetch failed the whole script died, taking the sidebar and elevation
ribbon with it, not just the map.

Fixed:

- Leaflet 1.9.4 JS and CSS inlined, BSD-2-Clause attribution retained. Verified:
  `leafletInlined: true`, no `script[src*=leaflet]` in the document.
- Google Fonts made non-render-blocking via `media="print" onload="this.media='all'"`
  with a `<noscript>` fallback, so an unreachable host cannot stall first paint.
  Every font stack already ended in a generic family, so appearance degrades cleanly.
- Tile failure is now detected and surfaced rather than leaving a blank grey square.

Reproduction of the fix: a copy of the page with every fetchable external host
repointed at an unroutable address (`127.0.0.1:9`), confirmed to have zero reachable
external URLs, then loaded:

```
leafletLoaded: true      mapExists: true       markers: 29
routeDrawn: 1            stopCards: 29         ribbonTicks: 29
popupWorks: true         filterWorks: 6        vanNotes: 6
offlineNoteShown: true
offlineNoteText: "Map tiles need a connection. The route, stops and elevation all still work offline."
```

Only the tile imagery is lost with no signal. The route, all 29 stops, the elevation
profile, popups and filters all keep working.

### C2. The drawn polyline is 146.82 km, not the 148.6 km stated. RECORDED, NOT CHANGED

Re-derived by haversine over the 678 stored points:

```
re-derived polyline length = 146.82 km   (page claims 148.6)
TOTAL_KM (elevation axis)  = 148.64
```

The 148.64 figure comes from the unsimplified geometry and matches published
figures, so the headline number is defensible. The RDP simplification cut 1.8 km of
corners, so the drawn route disagrees with its own elevation axis and with every POI
`km` value by about 1.2%. Not user-visible, and correcting it would mean shipping
the unsimplified polyline at a real page-weight cost. Recorded so it is not
rediscovered as a mystery later.

### C3. Nevins Lookout was dropped as unverifiable, and is in OSM. FIXED

The prior session dropped it because coordinates could not be confirmed. Overpass

route. Re-added at km 112.4 with verified coordinates.

### C4. Moki Tunnel clearance was omitted, and OSM carries it. FIXED

The prior session left the height out because sources disagreed. The OSM way for
the tunnel (`way/110680672`) is unambiguous:

```

maxspeed = 20        tunnel:name = Moki Tunnel
```

For a high-roof Sprinter this is the most load-bearing single fact on the page, and
it was the first question the probe asked. Now carried both in the Moki Tunnel POI
and in the van essentials panel at the top of the page.

### C5. "1 Tunnel" became ambiguous once a second tunnel was added. FIXED

Introduced by this session's own work, caught by re-deriving the hero stats after
the POI set changed rather than carrying the count forward:

```

```

Moki is the one the highway drives through; Aukopae is a former rail tunnel you walk
to. The stat was defensible but a careful reader counts two. Label changed to
`1 Road tunnel`.

## Performance and robustness

- `drawRibbon` rebuilt all SVG on every `resize` event with no debounce. On mobile
  the URL bar showing and hiding fires this continuously. Now debounced at 120 ms.
- `.ribbon-readout` used `transform:translateX(-50%)` with the first tick at
  `translate(0.0,32.0)`, so the Stratford readout was half clipped off the left
  edge. Now clamped to the container at both ends. Measured at 375 px wide:
  `readoutLeftEdge: 38 (not clipped)`, `readoutRightEdge: 337 (not clipped)`.
- POI `name` and `desc` were interpolated into `innerHTML` unescaped. Static data,
  so not a live vector, but it silently constrained what future content could
  contain. An `esc()` helper now escapes all five HTML metacharacters on every
  interpolation. Relevant now that the next task is adding POI categories.
- The Leaflet `<script>` was render-blocking with no `defer`. Now inlined, so there
  is no blocking network fetch at all.

### Defects found and fixed during this rebuild

**D1. The whole page rendered 93 px wider than a 375 px phone, and the overflow
was silently clipped. FIXED.**
`.topbar` is a grid item of `.shell` and so had `min-width:auto`, which resolves
to its min-content width. Its `white-space:nowrap` title and strapline gave it a
min-content of 468 px, which sized the grid column to 468 px on a 375 px
viewport. `html{overflow:hidden}` then clipped the right-hand 93 px, taking the
Map button with it, with no scrollbar to reveal it. Reproduction, before the fix:

    innerWidth 375, documentElement.clientWidth 375, but
    .topbar 468, .stage 468, .stop-list 468, body.scrollWidth 468

Isolated by hiding each grid child in turn and re-measuring the column:
hiding `.topbar` collapsed it to 375, hiding `.ribbonbar` or `.stage` did not.
Fixed with `.shell > *{ min-width:0 }`. After: every element 375, Map button
right edge at 364, `body.scrollWidth` 375.

Note this defect is invisible to a reading audit and to `documentElement.scrollWidth`,
which reported 0 overflow throughout because the clipping is what hides it.

**D2. Eight ribbon ticks were half-clipped at the two ends. FIXED.**
`x = km => (km/TOTAL_KM)*w` puts km 0 at cx=0 and km 148.6 at cx=w, so a 4.5 px
radius circle lost half its area to the SVG edge. Four stops sit at km 0.0 to
0.1 and four at km 148.5 to 148.6, so eight ticks were affected. Fixed by
clamping cx into `[R, w-R]`.

**D3. The co-located tick fan could push a tick out of the box entirely. FIXED.**
The fan direction was chosen by `cy < h/2` without checking that the group fitted
in that direction. Measured `cyRange` max of 97.1 in a 96 px box, so at least one
tick was drawn below the baseline and clipped. Fixed by fanning towards whichever
side has room and shrinking the step to fit. After: all 38 circles inside the
box, cx 4.5 to 348.5, cy 18.5 to 91.5, zero outside.

**D4. The main content had no headings. FIXED.**
All 8 headings on the page were inside the drawer. The 38 stop names were `<p>`,
so no assistive technology could navigate the stop list, which is the entire
content of the page. Promoted to `<h2 class="stop-name">`; the existing class
already carried the type styles, so nothing moved visually (font-size still
16 px). Now 38 `h2` in `main`.

**D5. Ribbon ticks were 9x9 touch targets. FIXED.**
Below WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8's 24x24 minimum on a page whose stated priority is
mobile. Added a transparent r=12 hit circle inside each tick group, ordered after
the category fill rules so it cannot pick up a colour, and excluded from the
`:focus` radius change. Measured target now 24x24.

**D6. Seasonal closure was flagged on one stop and not on its two siblings. FIXED.**
Mount Damper Falls carried a `warning`; Lauren's Lavender Farm (October to
mid-May) and the Forgotten World Adventures depot (October to May) stated their
season only in body prose despite being equally shut in September, which is when
Ross is going. Both now carry warnings. Three warnings render.

**D7. Filter counts were hardcoded in the markup.** Six typed integers that would
silently drift on the next POI change, which is the exact failure the project's
own standing rule warns about. Now derived from `POIS` at load.

### The climb figures were method-dependent, and one method was indefensible

Investigating Moki exposed two defects in the original `fwh-walk-metrics.py`, which
produced every walk figure now on the page.

**Defect A: fixed sample count, so spacing varied with track length.** Line 43:

```python
def resample(pts, n=90):
```

Every track was resampled to 90 points regardless of length. An 0.87 km walk got about
10 m spacing; the 11.8 km Moki Track got about 130 m. Cumulative ascent is acutely
sensitive to sample spacing, so **the gain figures were not comparable with each
other**, and the long tracks were the ones most understated.

**Defect B: disjoint ways concatenated into one elevation profile.** Line 76:

```python
groups[nm]["pts"].extend(pts)
```

All OSM ways sharing a name were concatenated in arbitrary order and the elevation
profile was computed along the joined sequence. Each way boundary is a spatial jump,
and every jump was counted as a climb. Moki Track had 7 ways, so 6 phantom climbs.

**Then the fix itself was wrong, and the test caught it.** Re-deriving at a fixed 25 m
ground interval gave Moki 1598 m of ascent across an altitude range of only 108 m. That
is not plausible terrain. A spacing sweep over the identical geometry:

```
 spacing  samples    gain m   gain/km
    25 m      723      1598      88.6
    50 m      362      1345      74.6
   100 m      182      1011      56.1
   150 m      122       660      36.6
   200 m       92       591      32.8
   300 m       62       467      25.9
```

**Cumulative gain does not converge as spacing shrinks.** Real terrain climb converges;
a quantity that keeps growing as you sample finer is dominated by the elevation model's
own noise and interpolation artefacts. Open-Meteo serves a roughly 90 m model, and DOC's
own nodes sit at 117 m spacing, so 25 m sampling was inventing climb.

Resolution: sample at **100 m**, the model's own resolution, and label the figure
approximate on the page and in the drawer. Distance and the altitude range are not
affected by this and remain solid.

A flat-water control over Lake Taupo returned 0.0 m of phantom gain at both 100 m and
25 m spacing. **That control is inconclusive and should not be cited as evidence**: DEMs
flatten lakes hydrologically, so a lake cannot exhibit the artefact. It proves only that
the API is deterministic. The non-convergence above is the actual evidence.

**Defect C, mine, caught before shipping.** The rewrite computed round-trip ascent as
`gain * 2`. That is only correct when a track's ascent equals its descent. Joshua
Morgan's Grave is +9 m / -0 m one way, so its true return climb is 9 m and the formula
gave 18 m. Correct formula for an out and back is `gain + loss`, which is what the
original script had used. Fixed before the numbers reached the page.
